# Words

One term per concept. Use these exactly. Every Filipino entry is a **draft translation**
and needs a native speaker and a lawyer to check it (open item M9).

| Concept | English | Filipino (draft) | Definition shown in the `?` popover (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| The one-page summary | brief | buod ng kaso | A one-page summary of your situation, made from what you wrote. You check it before any lawyer sees it. |
| A legal problem | matter | usapin | The legal problem a brief is about. |
| Free service | pro bono | libreng serbisyong legal (pro bono) | Legal help given free of charge. Lawyers must give some every few years under ULAS. |
| The SC programme | ULAS | ULAS | Rules on Unified Legal Aid Service: Supreme Court rules that require most lawyers to give 60 hours of free legal aid every 36 months. |
| Hours counted | ULAS hours | oras ng ULAS | Pro bono hours that count toward your 60-hour requirement. |
| Personal floor | personal minimum | personal na minimum | The 15 hours you must give yourself. Your firm's hours cannot replace them. |
| Lawyer status | verified lawyer | beripikadong abogado | A lawyer whose name and roll number we checked against the Supreme Court's list, on the date shown. |
| Clash | conflict of interest | salungatan ng interes | When a lawyer already acts for someone on the other side, or has a personal stake. The lawyer must decline. |
| Permission | consent | pahintulot | Your yes to a specific use of your information. You can say no to each item. |
| Hiding details | redaction | pagtatakip ng detalye | Hiding names, addresses and case numbers before the AI reads your text. |
| Notice | legal information, not legal advice | impormasyong legal, hindi payong legal | This explains the law in general. It is not advice for your case and does not make anyone your lawyer. |
| Distance | straight-line distance | tuwid na layo | The distance as the crow flies, not travel time by road. |
| Paid work | paid matter | may bayad na usapin | A matter you pay your lawyer for directly. APPogado takes no share. |

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| our lawyers | lawyers on APPogado | C6 |
| best, top, #1, featured, premium, sponsored | (nothing) | C2, C3 |
| pocket lawyer | — | Implies the app practices law |
| case (for a client's problem) | matter | "Case" implies a court filing |
| access gap, legal aid deficit | plain description | Jargon |
